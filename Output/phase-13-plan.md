# Plan: Phase 13 — Play songs from YouTube
Date: 2026-10-14
Derived from: Output/phase-13-spec.md. Review before building.

## Technical decisions (for you to approve)
- **No new packages.** YouTube's player script loads from YouTube; the search and playlist calls use plain `fetch` on YouTube's API.
- **You need a free YouTube API key** (Google Cloud console, "YouTube Data API v3"). It goes in `.env.local` as `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`. I'll tell you the steps when we reach step 2.
- **A player layer** in `src/app/library/song/[trackId]/play/`: a shared shape for what the game needs (the same things `usePlayer` returns today). `usePlayer.ts` (Spotify) keeps its code; a new `useYouTubePlayer.ts` returns the same shape. The game picks one by the song's source.
- **Song IDs don't change.** Saved songs and progress stay valid. YouTube matches live in `.data/video-matches.json` (`src/lib/video-matches.ts`, using `json-file.ts`); the app's own list in `.data/own-library.json` (`src/lib/own-library.ts`).
- **Risks to check first** (step 2): how to tell an ad from the song using the player's events; how fast pause and continue are for the line-end wait. If either fails, I stop and tell you before going on.

## 3. Matching songs to videos
- [x] Search with the title and artist, prefer Topic / auto-generated uploads, skip lyrics-video words, show the top 3 with warnings.
- [x] Save your pick; "Wrong video" swaps it.
- [x] Per-song source: YouTube if matched, else Spotify; a setting to force one.

- Done (2026-10-14): `src/lib/youtube.ts` (search and ranking rules), `video-matches.ts` (saved in `.data/video-matches.json`), routes `/api/video/[trackId]` (read, save) and `/api/video/[trackId]/search`. Song screen has a "Music from" control, YouTube or Spotify, and a picker with three videos, each with a warning when it looks like a lyrics video, another version, or a different length; Topic uploads rank first. In the game, "Wrong video?" above the corner player leads back to the song screen to pick another. Your choice of source is remembered in this browser; with no choice, a song with a video plays on YouTube, else Spotify. 11 new checks (63 in all); lint and build pass.
- Tried for real (2026-10-14): a search for "Never Gonna Give You Up" returned the Topic upload first, the official video second, and a "Lyrics" channel third (flagged after a fix: lyrics channels count even when the title doesn't say it); saving and reading a choice works, and a bad video id is refused. The test entry was deleted from `.data/`. The song screen and the game screens themselves need your Spotify login, so I haven't seen them: how the picker looks, and where the corner player and "Wrong video?" sit over the player bar, are for your play-test.
- First play-test (2026-10-14, "Bloody Mary", Lady Gaga): the chosen Topic upload showed "This video can't be played here" in the corner player, though YouTube's search had flagged it embeddable. Cause: very likely blocked in your country. A search with country ES drops that video and offers other Topic uploads (US and MX still list it). Fixes: the picker now sends your browser's country and drops videos blocked there or not public; the error panel offers "Choose another video" and shows YouTube's error number; the "Wrong video?" link now sits at the player's left edge (your browser extension covered its right end). The corner player and its place above the player bar looked right.
- Second play-test (2026-10-14): the new choice failed too (error 150). Tried on a temporary page: the two "Bloody Mary" Topic uploads load with error 150 about half a second after loading, even without playing, though YouTube's search says they can be embedded. So "Use this video" now checks first: it loads the video in a visible player (not playing) and saves only if no error comes within 1.2 seconds. Tested end to end: both Topic uploads refused ("This one can't be played here. Try another."), the third (LatinHype's upload) accepted and saved. Universal Music songs may often need a non-Topic upload.
- **Real cause found (2026-10-14, after "no video works"):** YouTube refuses to play on a page whose address is a number. The app runs at `http://127.0.0.1:3000` because Spotify's login needs it; every video, even ones that play on `localhost`, answered error 150 there. Tested on the pane with four player settings (no origin, privacy host, and so on): all failed on `127.0.0.1`. Fix: on a numbered address the player runs inside a small page served from `localhost` (`src/app/yt-frame/page.tsx`), shown in the game page and driven by messages (`createPlayer` in `youtubeApi.ts`; the picker's check and the game both use it). Tested on `127.0.0.1`: Rick Astley's video plays with its time, length and id passed across, pause holds the time, and the Bloody Mary Topic upload correctly still says error 150 (that one really is blocked). That page is a full web page: it only answers the page that opened it, and only from this machine.
- Third play-test (2026-10-14, "El Mar de Sus Ojos", Carlos Vives): the song played but no corner player showed. Your saved choice was on disk, so the play page wasn't seeing it: the page and the save route can each hold their own copy of the saved matches in memory while the dev server runs, and the page's copy was out of date, so it played the song on Spotify. Fix: the play page now reads the matches file every time (`getMatch` in `video-matches.ts`). A rule for later: data written by an API route and read by a page needs a fresh read, not the store's memory. Not seen on your account yet.
- Also fixed: the corner player was 320 × 180, under YouTube's 200-pixel minimum height; it's now 356 × 200 (spec updated).
- Changed from the spec: "Wrong video" doesn't swap in place; it opens the song screen's picker (your game in progress isn't kept). "Play again" on the home page doesn't know your source choice, so it follows the saved video (YouTube) if the song has one.

## 4. Own library list
Built in three parts, each its own commit (4a behind the scenes, 4b the library, 4c the add boxes).
- [x] The app's own list, started from your cached Spotify library (no new Spotify requests). *Decision 2026-10-14: your Spotify lists keep coming from their saved copy in the "From Spotify" tab, unchanged; the app's own list holds what comes from YouTube. Making the library work without a Spotify login is step 5.*
- [x] Import a pasted YouTube playlist (public or unlisted).
- [x] "Add a song": a YouTube link, or a title and artist.
- [x] Library and playlist pages read from the own list; Spotify playlists still show as before.
- **4a done (2026-10-14), behind the scenes:** a song added from YouTube gets the id `yt-<video id>` (`track-id.ts`); every route that checked for a Spotify id now accepts both (games, saved, lyrics, lyrics check, video). `own-library.ts` keeps added songs and playlists in `.data/own-library.json`. The lyrics route finds an added song in that file instead of asking Spotify. `youtube.ts` reads pasted links, a video's details, and a playlist (up to 200 songs, public or unlisted), and works out a title and artist from a video. Routes: `/api/own/add` (a link or a picked video), `/api/own/search` (title and artist), `/api/own/lists`. Saved choices and the own list are read from their files each time (`freshJsonFile`). Tried against the real YouTube: a video's details come back right, and a 181-song playlist reads in. Known limit: a title like "Song - Artist" is read as "Artist - Song", so those songs show as having no synced lyrics; Topic uploads are clean. 17 new checks.
- **4b and 4c done (2026-10-14), what you see:** a third tab in the library, **From YouTube** (`/library?tab=youtube`). It has two ways to add: paste a link (one video adds a song; a public or unlisted playlist adds up to 200 songs, leaving out ones YouTube says can't play in your country), or type a title and artist, get three videos, and pick one (checked to play before it's added). Added songs and imported playlists show as cards, open like any list (search, filters, heart and bookmark), and also come up in the whole-library search. A song added from YouTube can only play from YouTube (the Spotify button is greyed on its screen). The video picker and the check were split into `VideoChoices.tsx` so both the song screen and the add box use them. What I tried: the own library's add, list and search behaviour on a temporary route (new lists, repeats skipped, newest first), and the YouTube reading against the real service. **Not seen on screen** (the library pages need your Spotify login): how the new tab and add box look, and adding for real.
- Limits worth knowing: a playlist of music videos with "Song - Artist" titles gets swapped names (see above); videos YouTube flags as blocked in your country are left out, but a few that look fine can still fail at play time, and then "Choose another video" on the song screen fixes it.
