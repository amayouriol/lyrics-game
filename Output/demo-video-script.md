# Demo video: a 90-second script for Lyric Drop
Date: 2026-10-15

A short screen recording for your portfolio or to send people. It shows how the game works without putting anyone's lyrics, accounts or keys on show.

## Before you record
- **Use the app without Spotify** ("Or use the app without Spotify" on the start page), with a few songs from the **From YouTube** tab. That keeps Spotify's name, logo and your account out of the video.
- **Clean window.** A new Chrome window (or profile) with no other tabs, bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off (Windows: Focus assist). Zoom 100%.
- **No secrets on screen.** Don't open `.env.local`, the Google Cloud page or the terminal with your keys.
- **Sound.** If you record the music, the video may get a copyright claim on YouTube or other sites. Safer: record with no system audio and add a royalty-free track in the editor, or keep clips of the game under 10 seconds and upload as unlisted. Pick songs you don't mind muting.
- **Tool.** Windows has a recorder built in: press `Win + G`, then the Capture button (record). Or use OBS (free). Record at 1920 × 1080.

## Shots (about 90 seconds)
1. **Start page, 5 s.** Show the name and the line "Turn your playlist into karaoke."
2. **Add songs, 15 s.** Library, then the From YouTube tab. Paste a playlist link, or search a title and artist and pick a video. Say or write: "Any song on YouTube."
3. **Set up the game, 10 s.** Open a song: choose Type or Sing, pick a level.
4. **Play, 30 s.** The music starts, the lyrics move, blanks drop out. Type a word, show the points and the streak, show the song waiting for a word. Keep the corner player visible: it is YouTube's own.
5. **Results, 10 s.** The end screen: score, accuracy, your guesses.
6. **Home, 10 s.** Your totals and "Words that keep dropping."
7. **End card, 10 s.** Text on screen: "Built with Next.js, React and TypeScript. YouTube's official player. Lyrics from LRCLIB." and the GitHub link (https://github.com/andrea2220896-max/lyrics-game).

## What to say (one line each)
- "Lyric Drop hides words from a song's lyrics, and you fill them in as it plays."
- "Songs come from YouTube's official player, so I don't host any music."
- "I built it step by step with a written plan for each phase, and tested every phase on real use."
